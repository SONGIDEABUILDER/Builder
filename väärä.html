📖 HOW TO USE SONG IDEA BUILDER

What Is This?

SONG IDEA BUILDER turns your rough song idea into a complete, production-ready Suno AI prompt package — including structure, lyrics, style tags, BPM, chord mood, and vocal direction. Reddit creators use Suno as a sketchpad before heading to a studio. This tool bridges the gap between "I have a vibe" and "I have a full prompt."

Quick Start

1
Tick the rules checkbox in the red box at the top of the page. The rest of the app stays locked until you confirm you've read it.
2
Add your free Google Gemini API key. Get one at aistudio.google.com/apikey — no credit card needed. Your key stays in your browser and is sent only to Google's API, never anywhere else.
3
Describe your song concept. Be as specific or as vague as you like. The AI fills in the gaps. Example: "A bittersweet indie pop song about leaving your hometown and never quite fitting in anywhere else."
4
Pick your genre, mood, vocals, and BPM. These shape the entire output. You can leave things blank and the AI will decide.
5
Hit Generate. Gemini analyzes your inputs and returns a full package in seconds.
6
Copy the Suno-Ready Prompt and paste it directly into suno.com. Use the Style Tags in Suno's style field.

What You Get

  • Suno-Ready Prompt — a single optimised block you paste into Suno's song description
  • Song Blueprint — structure, key/chord mood, BPM range, vocal style, instruments
  • Style Tags — Suno-compatible genre/mood/instrument tags
  • Formatted Lyrics — verse/pre-chorus/chorus/bridge with [labels] Suno understands
💡 Pro tip: In Suno, paste the formatted lyrics into the "Lyrics" box and the style tags into the "Style of Music" box. Enable "Custom Mode" first for best results.

Privacy

Your API key is stored only in your browser's memory for the session. Nothing is logged or stored server-side. All Gemini API calls go directly from your browser to Google. Note: Google's free tier uses your prompts and outputs to improve their models — if that matters to you, this isn't the tool for sensitive lyrics, but it's a non-issue for a song idea generator.
