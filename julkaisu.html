How to Use Song Idea Builder

What Is This?

SONG IDEA BUILDER turns your rough song idea into a complete, production-ready Suno AI prompt package — including structure, lyrics, style tags, BPM, chord mood, and vocal direction. This tool bridges the gap between "I have a vibe" and "I have a full prompt."

Quick Start

1
Tick the rules checkbox. The rest of the app stays locked until you confirm you've read it.
2
Add your free Google Gemini API key. Get one at aistudio.google.com/apikey — no credit card needed. Your key is only sent to Google's API.
3
Describe your song concept. Be as specific or vague as you like. Example: "A bittersweet indie pop song about leaving your hometown and never quite fitting in anywhere else."
4
Pick genre, mood, vocals, and BPM. These shape the entire output. Leave any blank and the AI decides.
5
Hit Generate. Gemini returns a full package in seconds.
6
Copy the Suno-Ready Prompt and paste it directly into suno.com. Use the Style Tags in Suno's style field.

What You Get

  • Suno-Ready Prompt — a single optimised block you paste into Suno's song description
  • Song Blueprint — structure, key/chord mood, BPM range, vocal style, instruments
  • Style Tags — Suno-compatible genre/mood/instrument tags
  • Formatted Lyrics — verse/pre-chorus/chorus/bridge with [labels] Suno understands
💡 Pro tip: In Suno, paste the formatted lyrics into the "Lyrics" box and the style tags into the "Style of Music" box. Enable "Custom Mode" first for best results.

Privacy

Your API key is stored only in your browser's memory for the session. Nothing is logged or stored server-side. All Gemini API calls go directly from your browser to Google. Note: Google's free tier uses your prompts and outputs to improve their models.
